ARC Clinical
Browser Demo

This is a public demonstration of an internal tool used at the ALS Therapy Development Institute to review longitudinal clinical records collected from ARC Study participants via patient-directed SMART on FHIR.

All patient data shown is synthetic — randomly generated names, MRNs, dates, and clinical patterns. No real patient information is present.

What you can do:

  1. Browse the synthetic patient list in the left sidebar
  2. Click any patient to see their full clinical record
  3. Explore tabs for documents, problems, medications, labs/observations, encounters, allergies, immunizations, care plans, diagnostic reports, and goals
  4. Note the source: ccda and source: fhir tags showing where each record originated
  5. Use Export CSV on any tab to download that tab's data, or Export full record to download a single patient's complete dataset

About the real pipeline: ALS TDI is developing a reproducible pipeline that converts patient-directed SMART on FHIR retrievals into a researcher-friendly browser viewer, with the goal of enabling other patient registries to operationalize their own SMART on FHIR data without requiring in-house data engineering. Learn more at als.net.
